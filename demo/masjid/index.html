About us

A community-run masjid, open to all.

Masjid An-Noor has served local families for over two decades, offering daily congregational prayers, weekend Islamic classes for children, and a welcoming space for new Muslims and visitors of every background.

Our imam and volunteer board run weekly halaqas, community iftars during Ramadan, and a small food-assistance program for neighbors in need.

Daily congregation~150 worshippers
Jummah attendance~400 worshippers
Weekend schoolSat & Sun, 10am–1pm
Established1998
