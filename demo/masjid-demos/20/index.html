About us

Built by the community, for the community.

What began as a handful of families praying together has grown into one of Kingsford's most active community spaces, thanks entirely to local support.

01

Five Daily Prayers

Congregational prayers held on time, every day, led by our resident imam.

02

Weekend Islamic School

Qur'an, Arabic, and Islamic studies classes for children every Saturday and Sunday.

03

Community Support

A small food-assistance program and counseling referrals for neighbors in need.

04

New Muslim Care

A dedicated welcome program pairing new Muslims with a mentor from the community.
