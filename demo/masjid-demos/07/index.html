About us

Faith, family, and community — under one roof.

“Our doors are open to everyone — lifelong members, new Muslims, and neighbors who just want to understand who we are.”

— Imam Younus Habib, Resident Imam

Masjid Al-Salaam exists to serve Harborview and the surrounding area with a clean, well-maintained space for prayer, learning, and community gathering.

The board and volunteers keep the doors open seven days a week, funded entirely by the community members who walk through them.
