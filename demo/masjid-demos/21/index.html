About us

A community-run masjid, open to all.

Masjid Al-Hidayah has served local families for over two decades, offering daily congregational prayers, weekend Islamic classes for children, and a welcoming space for new Muslims and visitors of every background.

We host weekly halaqas, a busy weekend school, and seasonal community events — all run by volunteers from within the congregation.

Daily congregation~80 worshippers
Jummah attendance~330 worshippers
