About us

Serving worshippers of every background.

Islamic Center of Cedar Falls has served local families for over two decades, offering daily congregational prayers, weekend Islamic classes for children, and a welcoming space for new Muslims and visitors of every background.

We host weekly halaqas, a busy weekend school, and seasonal community events — all run by volunteers from within the congregation.

Daily congregation~120 worshippers
Jummah attendance~300 worshippers
Weekend schoolSat & Sun, 10am–1pm
Established1995
