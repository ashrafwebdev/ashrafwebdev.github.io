About us

Built by the community, for the community.

Sunnydale Masjid has served local families for over two decades, offering daily congregational prayers, weekend Islamic classes for children, and a welcoming space for new Muslims and visitors of every background.

We host weekly halaqas, a busy weekend school, and seasonal community events — all run by volunteers from within the congregation.

Daily congregation~200 worshippers
Jummah attendance~450 worshippers
Weekend schoolSat & Sun, 10am–1pm
Established1988
