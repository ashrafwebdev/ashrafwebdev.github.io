Our history

Faith, family, and community — under one roof.

Since opening, Masjid Al-Furqan has grown from a small prayer room into a full community hub — five daily prayers, weekend school, and regular programs for every age group.

1977

Masjid Al-Furqan opens its doors, holding its first Jummah prayer for a small group of local families.

1985

Weekend Islamic school launches, and the community outgrows the original prayer hall.

1992

A renovation adds a dedicated sisters' section, classrooms, and a larger parking area.

Today

Beyond daily prayers, we run marriage counseling, new-Muslim support, and a food pantry that serves dozens of local families each month.
