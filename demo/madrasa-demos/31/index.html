Admissions FAQ

What ages do you accept?

We teach children from age 5 through adults, with classes grouped by age and level.

Do you offer online classes?

Yes — most programs are available online, in-person, or a hybrid of both.

Is there a sibling discount?

Families enrolling more than one child receive a reduced rate on tuition.

What if my child has no prior experience?

No problem — our foundations track starts from the very beginning.
