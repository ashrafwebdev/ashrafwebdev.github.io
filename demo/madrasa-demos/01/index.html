What we teach

Programs by age group

Children (5–12)

  • Qur'an FoundationsNoorani Qa'ida, tajweed basics, and short surah memorization in small groups.
  • Islamic StudiesStories of the prophets, basic fiqh, and character-building lessons.
  • Arabic ReadingLetter recognition through simple sentence reading, twice a week.

Teens (13–17)

  • Hifz TrackStructured memorization program with weekly revision testing.
  • Applied FiqhPractical rulings on worship, conduct, and daily life questions.
  • Conversational ArabicBuilding toward reading classical texts and everyday conversation.

Adults

  • Tajweed MasteryCorrecting recitation with certified ijazah-holding teachers.
  • Islamic Studies CircleWeekly evening halaqa covering aqeedah, seerah, and contemporary issues.
  • Arabic for AdultsFrom-scratch Arabic track for new learners, evenings and weekends.
