Getting started

What you'll need to enroll

Enrollment takes less than a week from inquiry to your child's first class — most families complete every step over a single phone call and a short form.

  • Student's name and age
  • Program or age group of interest
  • Preferred class schedule (weekday/weekend)
  • Any prior Qur'an or Arabic experience
  • Contact details for a placement call
