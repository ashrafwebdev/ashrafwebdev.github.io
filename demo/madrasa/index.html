What we teach

Programs by age group

Qur'an Foundations

Noorani Qa'ida, tajweed basics, and short surah memorization in small groups.

Islamic Studies

Stories of the prophets, basic fiqh, and character-building lessons.

Arabic Reading

Letter recognition through simple sentence reading, twice a week.

Hifz Track

Structured memorization program with weekly revision testing.

Applied Fiqh

Practical rulings on worship, conduct, and daily life questions.

Conversational Arabic

Building toward reading classical texts and everyday conversation.

Tajweed Mastery

Correcting recitation with certified ijazah-holding teachers.

Islamic Studies Circle

Weekly evening halaqa covering aqeedah, seerah, and contemporary issues.

Arabic for Adults

From-scratch Arabic track for new learners, evenings and weekends.
