Pinecrest & beyond

Relief and support, delivered directly to the families who need it.

Pinecrest Family Aid Society is a community-funded nonprofit delivering emergency relief and long-term support to families facing hardship, guided by the values of compassion and community.

Donate now See our programs

What we do

Our programs

Food Security

Weekly grocery boxes and hot meals for local families facing food insecurity.

Orphan Sponsorship

Monthly sponsorship connecting donors directly with orphaned children's education and care.

Elderly Care

Home visits, meal delivery, and companionship for isolated seniors.

Refugee Support

Resettlement assistance, housing help, and language classes for newly arrived refugees.
