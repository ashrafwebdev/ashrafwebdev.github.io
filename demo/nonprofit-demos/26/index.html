Al-Ansar Community Fund is a community-funded nonprofit delivering emergency relief and long-term support to families facing hardship, guided by the values of compassion and community.

Donate now See our programs

What we do

Our programs

Emergency Relief

Rapid response aid — food, shelter, and essentials — delivered within 48 hours of a crisis.

Refugee Support

Resettlement assistance, housing help, and language classes for newly arrived refugees.

Disaster Response

Volunteer teams deployed to help communities recover after floods, storms, and fires.

Food Security

Weekly grocery boxes and hot meals for local families facing food insecurity.
