What we do

Our programs

Clean Water Access

Wells and water filtration systems built in underserved communities abroad.

Education Grants

Scholarships and school supplies for students from low-income households.

Food Security

Weekly grocery boxes and hot meals for local families facing food insecurity.

Refugee Support

Resettlement assistance, housing help, and language classes for newly arrived refugees.
