Maple Ridge & beyond

Relief and support, delivered directly to the families who need it.

Al-Ma'un Charitable Trust is a community-funded nonprofit delivering emergency relief and long-term support to families facing hardship, guided by the values of compassion and community.

What we do

Our programs

Education Grants

Scholarships and school supplies for students from low-income households.

Youth Mentorship

After-school mentoring and life-skills programs for at-risk youth.

Disaster Response

Volunteer teams deployed to help communities recover after floods, storms, and fires.

Refugee Support

Resettlement assistance, housing help, and language classes for newly arrived refugees.
