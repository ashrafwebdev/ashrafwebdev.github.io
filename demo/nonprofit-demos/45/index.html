Brookhaven & beyond

Built by neighbors, for neighbors — and for families further afield.

Brookhaven Humanitarian Network is a community-funded nonprofit meeting basic needs today while investing in the education and opportunity that break cycles of poverty.

Donate now See our programs

What we do

Our programs

Emergency Relief

Rapid response aid — food, shelter, and essentials — delivered within 48 hours of a crisis.

Clean Water Access

Wells and water filtration systems built in underserved communities abroad.

Youth Mentorship

After-school mentoring and life-skills programs for at-risk youth.

Education Grants

Scholarships and school supplies for students from low-income households.
