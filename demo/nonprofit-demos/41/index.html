Northgate & beyond

Relief and support, delivered directly to the families who need it.

Northgate Family Support Network is a community-funded nonprofit delivering emergency relief and long-term support to families facing hardship, guided by the values of compassion and community.

What we do

Our programs

Orphan Sponsorship

Monthly sponsorship connecting donors directly with orphaned children's education and care.

Disaster Response

Volunteer teams deployed to help communities recover after floods, storms, and fires.

Healthcare Access

Free clinics and medical aid for families who can't afford basic care.

Emergency Relief

Rapid response aid — food, shelter, and essentials — delivered within 48 hours of a crisis.
